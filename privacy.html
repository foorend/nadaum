MORE ME — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026 · Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. We don't hold your data

Every record in MORE ME (photos, activities, memos, stats, character) is stored only on your device. There is no account system. We do not collect, store, or transmit your records to any server we control.

2. Photos

Photos you take or select are processed on-device and automatically deleted after 24 hours. They are not sent to us. (When the PLUS AI feature is enabled, a photo is transiently sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service for activity recognition, then discarded without storage. The feature is off by default.)

3. Ads (free tier)

The free tier displays Google AdMob ads. Google may collect device identifiers for the purpose of serving ads — see Google's Privacy Policy. MORE ME does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permissions to track you across apps or websites.

4. Push notifications

Optional push notifications are delivered through OneSignal. To send a notification, OneSignal receives an anonymous device token from Apple and app-level tags we set (plus, entries, dots). These tags contain no personal information and cannot identify you. See OneSignal's Privacy Policy. You can revoke notification permission any time in Settings → Notifications → MORE ME.

5. Subscriptions

MORE ME PLUS subscriptions are processed by Apple. Subscription state is managed by RevenueCat on our behalf; RevenueCat receives your anonymous app user ID and Apple's subscription receipt to determine entitlement. RevenueCat does not receive your Apple ID or payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. You can cancel any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

6. Children

MORE ME is rated 4+ / Everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you control it fully. Use the "Reset everything" button in the Me tab to erase every record.
