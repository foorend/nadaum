Every day makes me more me.

A journal that watches, not judges. One photo per hour. Over 40 days, your 1,000-dot animal fills with the colors of the life you actually lived.

Download on the App Store Free · iOS 16+ · No account. No cloud.

Not a productivity app.

Five things it refuses to be.

No streaks.

Missed a day? A week? The animal just waits.

No goals.

You don't set a number. You just live.

No shame.

The app can't punish you. It doesn't have that button.

No cloud.

Photos stay on your device for 24h, then delete themselves. Nothing is uploaded.

No advice.

You get colors, not lectures. Interpret your own picture.

Ten stats. Not three.

Most journals reduce your life to Productivity, Wellness, and Social. That's a lie about how time actually works.

Cooking dinner isn't "rest" — it's Care + Create. Reading fiction isn't "learn" — it's Explore. Ten was the smallest number that stopped forcing us to lie.

Learn · Explore · Move · Care · Play · Work · Reflect · Create · Rest · Connect.

Why one photo per hour.

Because a day has 24 slots — no more, no less.

The one-hour cooldown between photos is the whole point. If you could take ten photos in a row, the journal would drift back into being about you performing for the app. One per hour keeps it about the hour.

The green button turns red. It counts down. When your hour is up, it turns green again. You take the photo, or you don't.

1,000 dots. 30 animals. One quiet mirror.
