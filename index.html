MORE ME

A self-growth journal where you photograph one hour of your day and a 1,000-dot animal fills in with the colors of your real life.

FAQ

Q. Where are my photos stored?
On your device only. They're kept for 24 hours so you can see them in the Today feed, then deleted automatically. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Q. Why only one photo per hour?
Because a day has 24 slots. One hour of your life becomes the raw material for one dot.

Q. What happens if I miss a day?
Nothing bad. MORE ME has no streaks, no goals, no shame. Your animal just waits. You only fill what you actually lived.

Q. Can I change my animal?
Once a year. The dots you've earned stay — they carry over to the new silhouette.

Q. What's MORE ME PLUS?
A subscription that removes all ads. Nothing else is gated — every feature stays free. It exists to support the app without changing what it is.

Q. How is the subscription billed?
Through your Apple ID. You can cancel any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. RevenueCat handles the entitlement logic.
